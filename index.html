About Us

Built on one rule: fix it like it's our own phone.

Ramzan Mobile Care was founded by Rashid Ramzan in Rawal Bazar, Vehari, with a simple promise — honest diagnosis, fair pricing, and repairs done in full view of the customer. What started as a single repair bench has grown into one of the area's most relied-upon mobile service points.

Every technician on the floor is trained on current-generation devices, and every part that goes into your phone is tested before and after fitting. If a repair isn't worth doing, we'll tell you that too.

  • Genuine & tested parts only
  • Free diagnostic checkup
  • Repairs done on-site, same day
  • 90-day repair warranty
